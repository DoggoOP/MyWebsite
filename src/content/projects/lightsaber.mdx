## Working prototype

I'm building a retractable lightsaber in Yoda's style, inspired by Hirotech's retractable saber work. The blade extends out of the hilt and lights up.

## How the blade works

The blade is a **magician's cane**, the coiled band a magician flicks out into a rigid stick. The LED strip and a wrap of glass cloth tape tension the cane so the extended blade stays straight and stiff.

- **Extending.** The coiled cane is held under tension, and a **clutch** releases the cane, which shoots out of the hilt in one motion.
- **Retracting.** One motor spools the cane back into the hilt. A second motor rotates the cane while the first one winds, because a cane wound straight in binds on the coils already inside.

## Packaging the mechanism

The clutch, both motors, the spool and the coiled blade all have to fit inside a hilt that still feels like a lightsaber in the hand. I used the CAD to fit the spool, motors and clutch into the internal volume and to keep the blade's exit path straight.
