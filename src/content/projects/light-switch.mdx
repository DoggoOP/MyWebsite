## Retrofit mechanism

I wanted automated lights without touching the building wiring, so the device works the existing switch the way a hand does. A **lead screw** drives a **guided carriage** across the switch, which turns the motor's rotation into a short, strong linear push in either direction. I laid out the travel, mounting clearance and alignment in CAD first, then designed the printed housing around that layout.

## Control

An **ESP32** drives the motor and reads a push button mounted beside the switch.

  <img src="/assets/light-switch-prototype.jpg" alt="Wall-mounted light-switch actuator wired to an ESP32 and push button" loading="lazy" />

The switch has run reliably since I finished the build, and I use the switch every day.
