## Why a modular wing

I designed this 1.8 m fixed-wing plane as an autonomous platform for monitoring sea-turtle poaching along Malaysia's coast, so the plane had to travel with me. A one-piece wing is awkward to print, pack and carry, so I split the airframe into printed sections that key together, which made the joints part of the design from the start.

## Designing a printed wing

This was my first 3D-printed wing. Open bays inside each panel remove plastic a solid print would carry, and the panel edges key into each other so the sections line up on assembly. The printed skin holds the airfoil shape; the bending load across the span belongs to a spar.

The flight tests taught me the main lesson of the project: in a printed wing, the spar comes first. Route a continuous spar through the panels, then remove material around that load path.
