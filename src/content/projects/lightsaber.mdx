## Design in progress

I'm designing a **retractable Yoda-inspired lightsaber** with Hirotech's retractable saber work as an inspiration. The current work is in CAD: defining the hilt's exterior, the upper assembly, and the space available for the deployment mechanism. The renders below show the design state, not a finished or tested prototype.

## Mechanical layout

The CAD views let me check how the exterior housing and internal volume line up from multiple directions. The next design work is to resolve the blade's travel path, the deployed and retracted stops, and how the mechanism can be assembled and serviced inside the hilt.

## Questions before fabrication

- How much deployment travel fits inside the hilt without making the grip too large?
- How should the moving parts be guided and stopped at both ends of travel?
- What clearances and material choices will keep the mechanism reliable after repeated cycles?
- Can the assembly be opened for adjustment or repair without damaging the exterior?

The design is still being developed; deployment performance, durability, and fabrication method have not yet been validated.
